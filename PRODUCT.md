# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Early-career engineering teams, hiring managers, and project stakeholders who want a clear sense of Dimas Sholihulhadi as a dependable Web Developer and Software Engineer. They are evaluating his portfolio online and expect a polished, professional presentation that demonstrates technical skill and thoughtful software craftsmanship.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio site showcases Dimas as a capable Web Developer and Software Engineer. It should communicate his practical experience, clean architecture mindset, and ability to build maintainable web applications. Success means visitors understand his strengths quickly, feel confident in his technical judgement, and can contact him without friction.

## Brand Personality

Professional, deliberate, calm.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI portfolio clichés like oversized gradient text, emoji-based icon sets, and noisy glassmorphism everywhere. Do not feel like a template or marketing brochure; avoid decorative flourishes that distract from the work and the engineering story.

## Design Principles

- Keep the interface calm and focused on content hierarchy: headline, supporting statement, evidence.
- Use restrained, modern visual language with clear rhythm and deliberate spacing.
- Favor vector iconography and consistent typography over decorative motif elements.
- Ensure every section feels coherent and usable on mobile first.
- Help visitors scan Dimas’s experience quickly without losing the sense of craft.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Design for readability and clarity: strong text contrast, responsive layout, and keyboard-friendly navigation. Respect reduced motion with non-essential animations disabled or simplified. Ensure links and buttons have clear labels and touch-friendly spacing.
